description: The immortal role that owns promoting releases to production.
tags: [orchestration, deploy]
charter: May promote a release from staging to production, and nothing else.

The role outlives its incumbent — sessions die, the captaincy does not.
Duties are bounded by the charter; every promotion runs the [[release-rite]]
and answers to the [[change-council]].
